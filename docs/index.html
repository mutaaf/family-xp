Up and running in 5 minutes

You need a Mac that stays home. That's the whole shopping list.

Get the folder. Download the ZIP, unpack it, and drag family-xp into your home folder.
Double-click install.command. If macOS hesitates: right-click → Open → Open. Answer one question — your family name.
Open your new address — like http://smithfamily.local/ — on any phone, tablet or laptop on your wifi. Hand the kids their sign-in cards.

🤖 Or just ask an AI. Paste the GitHub link into Claude and say "get me started" — the repo teaches the agent exactly what to do.

Questions parents ask

Is it safe for my kids? Everything runs and stays on your Mac — no cloud, no accounts, no tracking, no ads, no chat with strangers. Passcodes gate the portal; the trust model is "people in your house."
Does it need the internet? No. It's built to work with the internet down. (The read-aloud voice needs a one-time iOS voice download.)
What if a kid forgets their passcode? The Control Panel shows it, or "Redo setup" starts them fresh — XP and pages survive.
Can I change the prizes and chores? All of it, live, from the parent desktop: chores, cadences, prize prices, even the quest missions.
What does it cost? Nothing. Free, open source (MIT), no subscription — it's your computer doing the work.
